  1. 01 · IP Detection add-on

    Your IP policy, checked before any model runs

    With IP Detection, prompts and input images are checked against your policy in the app, workflows, API and agents. A blocked request never reaches a model.

    Optional IP Detection screens covered prompts, input images and video endpoint frames. It is a risk signal enforcing your own policy, not legal advice. Audio, 3D and finished images are excluded. The check bills separately in credits; a blocked request runs no generation and incurs no generation charge.

  2. 02 · Ownership and privacy

    Your work stays yours

    You own what you generate, from the first draft to the final asset. Under your enterprise agreement (MSA and DPA), nothing of yours trains a model without your prior written consent.

  3. 03 · Users and spend

    Unlimited users, one pool of credits

    On a twelve-month agreement with committed credits, add everyone who needs it. Set a spend cap per project and narrow its approved models (a project can narrow the organization's list, never widen it); every user, API key and agent in the project works inside them.
